  • LEAP-Block (JAMA): left bundle-branch area pacing beat right ventricular pacing on a composite driven by heart failure events (2.4% vs 12.5%).

  • PMR imaging recommendations: imaging is for intermediate pretest probability only; no single first-line modality.

1IgA nephropathy: four phase 3 reports in one week

  • Sibeprenlimab and atacicept final analyses add eGFR data: annualized slope about 0 vs -4.2 to -5.6 mL/min/1.73 m2/yr on placebo.
  • Povetacicept and ravulizumab are interim proteinuria analyses (UPCR down 52.0% and 46.6% vs 4.3% and 5.6%).

Caveat: All industry-funded; 24 to 104 weeks of follow-up; tiny event counts on composite end points; no standalone kidney failure data yet.

2LEAP-Block: LBBAP vs right ventricular pacing (JAMA)

  • 499 patients, AV block, LVEF 50% or higher, expected pacing burden over 40%.
  • Primary composite at 24 months: 2.4% vs 12.5% (HR 0.37, 95% CI 0.19-0.73).
  • HF events 3.4% vs 11.3%.

Caveat: Driven by HF events; no significant difference in death, biventricular upgrade, or device complications. 14 Chinese centers.

JAMA: PubMed | DOI | PMC

3AGA/AASLD guideline on primary biliary cholangitis

  • Suggests PBC-specific ANA when AMA-negative; UDCA first-line; liver stiffness for prognosis.
  • PPAR agonists for inadequate response or intolerance to UDCA; linerixibat for moderate-to-severe pruritus.
  • UDCA prophylaxis after liver transplantation.

Caveat: Certainty of evidence moderate to very low. Published in both Gastroenterology and Hepatology.

Method and limits

  • Source: PubMed E-utilities, entry date within the last 7 days. Query A: 30 internal medicine and subspecialty journals. Query B: publication type Practice Guideline or Guideline in internal medicine topics. Duplicates removed by PMID.
  • Screening and summaries: Claude, from abstracts and metadata only. Numbers are copied from abstracts; open the linked record before citing.
  • Full text: PMC records exist for 18 of 191 candidates. A PMC record does not guarantee machine-readable full text.
  • Excluded by scope: oncology (including ASCO living guidelines and NEJM lung and bladder trials), neurology and stroke, pediatrics, health policy, narrative and opinion pieces.
  • Not searched: preprints, society websites without PubMed indexing (ACC, ESC, KDIGO, IDSA, ADA living updates), FDA actions.
  • Badges describe the result against the primary end point: Positive, Negative, Neutral (no clear difference), Descriptive (observational, uncontrolled, or no efficacy test).
