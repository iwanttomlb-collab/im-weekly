At a glance

  • SHASTA-3/4 (NEJM): plozasiran cut triglycerides and acute pancreatitis (1 vs 5 per 100 patient-years) in severe hypertriglyceridemia.
  • PROGRESS (NEJM): transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) beat surveillance in moderate aortic stenosis with at-risk features (16.7% vs 23.9% death or heart failure (HF) event at 2 years), driven by HF events.
  • SIMPLAAFY (JAMA): after left atrial appendage closure (LAAC), aspirin alone and reduced-dose non-vitamin K oral anticoagulant (NOAC) were both noninferior to dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) at 6 months.
  • American Thoracic Society (ATS) guideline: strong recommendations for as-needed inhaled corticosteroid (ICS)-containing relievers at National Asthma Education and Prevention Program (NAEPP) steps 1-2 and ICS-formoterol maintenance-and-reliever therapy (MART) at steps 3-5.
  • Neutral or negative: hydralazine-isosorbide dinitrate in contemporary heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF) (H-HeFT), conservative kidney management vs dialysis preparation in frail older adults (no quality-adjusted life-year (QALY) difference).
  • Ertapenem-resistant, meropenem-susceptible Enterobacterales: higher 30-day mortality on imipenem or meropenem (odds ratio (OR) 2.24), challenging Infectious Diseases Society of America (IDSA) guidance.

Top 3 this week

1Plozasiran in severe hypertriglyceridemia (SHASTA-3 and SHASTA-4, NEJM)

  • 757 adults, 2:1 to plozasiran 25 mg or placebo at baseline and months 3, 6, 9.
  • Placebo-adjusted triglyceride change at 12 months: -45 and -47 percentage points; triglycerides below 500 mg/dL in 91% and 93% vs 51% and 50%.
  • Pooled acute pancreatitis: 1 vs 5 per 100 patient-years (rate ratio 0.22; P = 0.008).

Caveat: Funded by Arrowhead; primary end point is triglyceride change; pancreatitis from a pooled analysis over 12 months.

2PROGRESS: transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) vs clinical surveillance in moderate aortic stenosis (NEJM)

  • 744 patients with moderate aortic stenosis (AS) and at least one at-risk feature; mean age 78 years, mean valve area 1.2 cm2.
  • Death or heart failure (HF) event at 2 years: 16.7% vs 23.9% (risk difference -7.2 points, 95% confidence interval (CI) -13.1 to -1.3).
  • Death 8.4% vs 7.3%; HF events 10.8% vs 19.0%.

Caveat: Open-label; funded by Edwards Lifesciences; benefit driven by HF events, which are open to ascertainment bias; no mortality difference.

N Engl J Med: PubMed | DOI

3SIMPLAAFY: antithrombotic therapy after left atrial appendage closure (JAMA)

  • 1857 patients, 1:1:1 to dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) 6 months, reduced-dose non-vitamin K oral anticoagulant (NOAC) 3 months, or aspirin alone 12 months.
  • 6-month death, stroke, embolism or major bleeding: 6.9% DAPT vs 5.1% NOAC (relative risk (RR) 0.75) vs 3.3% aspirin (RR 0.49); both noninferior.
  • Device-related thrombus 3.4%, 0.9%, and 2.7%.

Caveat: Open-label; 6-month follow-up; uncomplicated left atrial appendage closure (LAAC) only; bleeding and thrombus outcomes exploratory.

New guidelines and statements

  • ATS - As-needed inhaled corticosteroids in asthma (clinical practice guideline) (Am J Respir Crit Care Med)
    • Six population, intervention, comparison, outcome (PICO) questions, Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation (GRADE); adolescents and adults across National Asthma Education and Prevention Program (NAEPP) steps 1-5.
    • Steps 1-2: strong for as-needed-only inhaled corticosteroid (ICS)-short-acting beta2-agonist (SABA) or ICS-formoterol over SABA only; conditional over scheduled ICS plus as-needed SABA.
    • Steps 3-5: strong for ICS-formoterol maintenance-and-reliever therapy (MART) over scheduled ICS-long-acting beta2-agonist (LABA) plus as-needed SABA; conditional for ICS+SABA reliever over SABA reliever on scheduled ICS-LABA.

  • AASLD (Expert Practice Opinion) - Diagnosis and management of hepatitis delta virus infection (Hepatology)
    • Test every hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg)-positive person at least once for hepatitis delta virus (HDV) antibody; confirm with HDV RNA.
    • Elastography-based fibrosis assessment; labs and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) surveillance every 6 months.
    • Compensated chronic HDV viremia: bulevirtide for suppression, or bulevirtide plus pegylated interferon (peg-IFN) alfa-2a for a finite course.

Method and limits

  • Source: PubMed E-utilities, entry date within the last 7 days. Query A: 30 internal medicine and subspecialty journals. Query B: publication type Practice Guideline or Guideline in internal medicine topics. Duplicates removed by PMID.
  • Screening and summaries: Claude, from abstracts and metadata only. Numbers are copied from abstracts; open the linked record before citing.
  • Full text: PMC records exist for 4 of 124 candidates. A PMC record does not guarantee machine-readable full text.
  • Excluded by scope: oncology, neurology and stroke, pediatrics (including the KALUMI malaria trial), surgery-only, health policy, narrative and opinion pieces. ACP Journal Club synopses in Annals were skipped because they carry no abstract.
  • Not searched: preprints, society websites without PubMed indexing (ACC, ESC, KDIGO, IDSA, ADA living updates), FDA actions.
  • Badges describe the result against the primary end point: Positive, Negative, Neutral (no clear difference), Descriptive (observational, uncontrolled, or no efficacy test).
