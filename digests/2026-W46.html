At a glance

  • Two large NEJM outcome trials were neutral: ziltivekimab (interleukin-6 (IL-6) inhibition, ZEUS) and pelacarsen (lipoprotein(a) lowering, Lp(a)HORIZON), with MACE hazard ratios (HR) of 0.99 and 1.02.
  • Systolic blood pressure (SBP) target below 120 mm Hg in type 2 diabetes: no MACE benefit in OPTIMAL-DIABETES (HR 0.90) and no cognitive benefit in BPROAD (HR 0.96).
  • OptimPacing (NEJM): left bundle-branch pacing beat right ventricular pacing in atrioventricular block (4-year composite 8.5% vs 19.2%; HR 0.41).
  • Older adults: stopping primary-prevention statins was noninferior at 2 years (STREAM), and holding the angiotensin-converting enzyme inhibitor (ACEi) or angiotensin receptor blocker (ARB) on the morning of non-cardiac surgery cut perioperative hypotension (31.2% vs 45.5%).
  • PEPPER (NEJM, n=18,883): aspirin was not noninferior to rivaroxaban or warfarin for venous thromboembolism (VTE) prophylaxis after hip or knee arthroplasty.
  • Guidelines: IgG4ward! Foundation treatment guidelines for IgG4-related disease (22 of 28 statements reached consensus) and a Korean autoimmune pancreatitis guideline.

Top 3 this week

1Anti-inflammatory and lipoprotein(a) strategies fail on outcomes (ZEUS and Lp(a)HORIZON, NEJM)

  • ZEUS: 6376 patients with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD), chronic kidney disease (CKD) and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hsCRP) of 2 mg/L or more; ziltivekimab 15 mg monthly lowered hsCRP by 64 percentage points vs placebo, but MACE was 17.6% vs 17.7% (HR 0.99; 95% CI 0.88 to 1.11) over median 33 months; serious infections 20.4% vs 17.9%.
  • Lp(a)HORIZON: 8322 patients with cardiovascular disease and Lp(a) of 70 mg/dL or more; pelacarsen 80 mg monthly lowered Lp(a) to 64.4 vs 233.4 nmol/L, but MACE was 12.3% vs 12.1% (HR 1.02; 95% CI 0.90 to 1.15) over median 4.7 years.
  • Lp(a) of 90 mg/dL or more: 12.3% vs 12.3% (HR 1.00).

Caveat: Funded by Novo Nordisk and Novartis; both large, adequately followed trials with a large biomarker effect, so a null result is informative for these mechanisms.

2SBP target below 120 mm Hg in type 2 diabetes (OPTIMAL-DIABETES, NEJM; BPROAD cognitive outcomes, BMJ)

  • OPTIMAL-DIABETES (Brazil, n=9476, open label): achieved SBP 121.8 vs 135.4 mm Hg; MACE 6.0% vs 6.7% (HR 0.90; 95% CI 0.77 to 1.06; P = 0.20) over median 4.0 years; serious adverse events 14.9% vs 14.6%.
  • BPROAD (China, 12,434 without probable dementia): dementia or mild cognitive impairment 3.69 vs 3.89 per 100 person-years (HR 0.96; 95% CI 0.86 to 1.06).
  • BPROAD MRI subset (n=862): smaller increase in white matter lesion volume with intensive treatment (difference -0.09 asinh cm3; P = 0.01).

Caveat: Both open label; BPROAD cognitive outcomes are prespecified secondary outcomes; MRI subset is small with attrition (605 at end of study).

3Left bundle-branch pacing vs right ventricular pacing for atrioventricular block (OptimPacing, NEJM)

  • 702 randomized, 685 in modified intention-to-treat; stopped early at the prespecified interim analysis.
  • Death, heart failure hospitalization or pacing-induced cardiomyopathy: 16 vs 35 events; 4-year cumulative incidence 8.5% vs 19.2% (HR 0.41; 95% CI 0.23 to 0.75; P = 0.003).
  • Serious adverse events unrelated to implantation: 11.8% vs 9.3% (P = 0.32).

Caveat: Investigator-initiated, open label; early termination with 51 total events inflates effect estimates; composite includes a softer component (pacing-induced cardiomyopathy).

New guidelines and statements

  • IgG4ward! Foundation - Treatment of IgG4-related disease (Lancet Rheumatol)
    • 62 contributors (52 physicians, 8 patients) from 15 countries; first dedicated treatment guideline since the 2015 consensus, prompted by two recent phase 3 placebo-controlled trials.
    • 40 questions, 28 treatment statements graded by Oxford Centre for Evidence-Based Medicine levels; consensus (more than 80% agreement) reached for 22 of 28.
    • Most statements still rest on low-level evidence; abstract does not list the individual recommendations.

    PubMed | DOI

  • Korean Pancreatobiliary Association - Clinical practice guideline for autoimmune pancreatitis (Gut Liver)
    • Covers diagnosis, steroid treatment, maintenance therapy, relapse management and follow-up.
    • Delphi consensus with graded evidence and recommendation strength, adapted to the Korean healthcare setting; abstract gives no specific recommendations.

    PubMed | DOI

  • AHA (scientific statement) - Cardiovascular health promotion for US veterans (Circulation)
    • Applies Life's Essential 8, expanded to mental health and substance use, to the veteran population.
    • Veterans in Veterans Affairs care often achieve outcomes equal to or better than non-VA counterparts; gaps remain in lifestyle, metabolic, lipid and blood pressure control, mental health engagement and sleep disorder detection.

    PubMed | DOI

  • Med Sci Sports Exerc (Task Force 9) - Care of athletes with cardiac channelopathies (Med Sci Sports Exerc)
    • Covers long QT, catecholaminergic polymorphic ventricular tachycardia, Brugada and short QT syndromes.
    • Moves away from blanket exclusion: return to play through shared decision-making after specialist assessment, risk stratification and treatment.

    PubMed | DOI

  • Fragility Fracture Network - International care model for vertebral fragility fracture (Calcif Tissue Int)
    • Adapted from an international Delphi consensus and national guidance, with knowledge-user survey input from 40 countries.
    • Three domains: pain management, improving physical functioning, and fall and fracture prevention (including vitamin D, calcium and osteoporosis medication initiation); defined key performance indicators.

Method and limits

  • Source: PubMed E-utilities, entry date within the last 7 days. Query A: 30 internal medicine and subspecialty journals. Query B: publication type Practice Guideline or Guideline in internal medicine topics. Duplicates removed by PMID.
  • Screening and summaries: Claude, from abstracts and metadata only. Numbers are copied from abstracts; open the linked record before citing.
  • Full text: PMC records exist for 12 of 154 candidates. A PMC record does not guarantee machine-readable full text.
  • Excluded by scope: oncology (including NCCN, ACS prostate screening, CSCO-ASCO nasopharyngeal and ALL MRD guidelines), neurology and stroke (including the NEJM trial of intensive blood pressure control after ischemic stroke), pediatrics (pediatric pulmonary hypertension, Fontan, type 1 diabetes immunotherapy and childhood angiitis guidance, EMPIRICAL), surgery-only, health policy, narrative and opinion pieces. The COURAGE extended follow-up in JAMA was skipped because it carries no abstract.
  • Not searched: preprints, society websites without PubMed indexing (ACC, ESC, KDIGO, IDSA, ADA living updates), FDA actions.
  • Badges describe the result against the primary end point: Positive, Negative, Neutral (no clear difference), Descriptive (observational, uncontrolled, or no efficacy test).
