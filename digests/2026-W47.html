At a glance

  • Obesity pharmacotherapy: ribupatide (GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist) cut weight 10.7% to 17.7% at 48 weeks in GEMINI-1 (JAMA); lower-dose CagriSema 1.7 mg and 1.0 mg each gave about 21% loss at 68 weeks (REDEFINE 9).
  • Finerenone in FSGS (FIND-CKD prespecified analysis): total eGFR slope improved by 1.3 mL/min/1.73 m2/yr and UPCR fell 41% vs placebo.
  • Coronary PCI: angiography-derived vFFR met noninferiority to wire FFR at 1 year (LIPSIA-STRATEGY); super-high-pressure balloons noninferior to intravascular lithotripsy for stent expansion (VICTORY); DECISION-CTO 10-year data show no difference.
  • Neutral or negative: upadacitinib in Takayasu arteritis (SELECT-TAK, primary end point missed), prediction-aided bDMARD tapering in RA (PATIO), diabetes-educated psychologist for HbA1c in type 1 diabetes.
  • MSG-ERC and ECMM published five consensus papers revising clinical trial end points for invasive fungal disease; IPD meta-analysis puts 1-year invasive aspergillosis mortality at 53.8%.
  • Guidance: ACC scientific statement on earlier lipid lowering, AHA statement on PAD in acute care, UKKA guideline on AF anticoagulation in advanced CKD, AST IDCOP peritransplant infection update.

Top 3 this week

1Incretin-based obesity trials: ribupatide (GEMINI-1, JAMA) and lower-dose CagriSema (REDEFINE 9)

  • Mechanism (ribupatide): dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic polypeptide/glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, once weekly subcutaneous.
  • GEMINI-1: 567 adults in China without diabetes; weight change at 48 weeks -10.7%, -16.4%, -17.7% with 2, 4, 6 mg vs -1.4% placebo; placebo-adjusted -9.3, -15.0, -16.3 percentage points.
  • REDEFINE 9: 309 adults without diabetes, mean BMI 39.1; weight change at 68 weeks -20.7% (1.7 mg each) and -21.0% (1.0 mg each) vs -2.0% pooled placebo (efficacy estimand).
  • At least 5% weight loss: 85.7% to 88.0% with ribupatide 4 to 6 mg vs 16.0%; 95.1% and 96.3% with CagriSema vs 33.4%.

Caveat: REDEFINE 9 funded by Novo Nordisk; GEMINI-1 limited to adults in China with mean age 34.4 years; gastrointestinal adverse events 47.9% to 63.1% with ribupatide vs 23.1%; no outcome data.

2Finerenone in focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FIND-CKD prespecified analysis, JASN)

  • 215 participants with FSGS (half primary, half secondary), mean eGFR 45, median UPCR 1427 mg/g, on stable RAS inhibition.
  • Total eGFR slope improved by 1.3 mL/min/1.73 m2/yr vs placebo (95% CI 0.5-2.2); consistent in biopsy-proven FSGS (1.4) and in primary vs secondary disease.
  • UPCR down 41% at month 12; kidney composite HR 0.58 (95% CI 0.30-1.15).
  • Hyperkalemia-related adverse events 15% vs 20%.

Caveat: Subgroup of a larger nondiabetic CKD trial; composite end point not significant; proteomics analysis post hoc.

J Am Soc Nephrol: PubMed | DOI

3Coronary physiology and lesion preparation: LIPSIA-STRATEGY and VICTORY (JACC)

  • LIPSIA-STRATEGY: 1053 patients with intermediate stenoses; 1-year MACE 3.0% with angiography-derived vFFR vs 4.1% with wire FFR (risk difference -1.01 percentage points; P for noninferiority <0.001).
  • vFFR led to more revascularization of the study lesion (41.2% vs 35.5%).
  • VICTORY: 278 patients with calcified lesions; stent expansion 85% with super-high-pressure noncompliant balloons vs 84% with intravascular lithotripsy (P noninferiority <0.0001); 30-day TVF 2.8% vs 5.7% (HR 0.50; 95% CI 0.15-1.66).

Caveat: LIPSIA event rate lower than anticipated; post hoc relative-margin sensitivity analysis failed noninferiority in mITT. VICTORY uses an imaging surrogate end point, experienced operators with mandatory OCT, 30-day clinical follow-up.

New guidelines and statements

    • Supports earlier identification and more sustained treatment based on lifetime LDL-C burden, inherited and acquired risk enhancers, and subclinical atherosclerosis imaging.
    • Imaging-detected atherosclerosis may identify people whose risk calculators underestimate.

Cardiology and cardiometabolic

PositiveRCT, noninferiorityJ Am Coll Cardiol

Angiography-derived vFFR vs wire FFR to guide revascularization (LIPSIA-STRATEGY)

  • n=1053, intermediate stenoses, acute or chronic coronary syndromes, 8 German centers.
  • 1-year MACE 3.0% vs 4.1% (relative risk 0.75; 95% CI 0.39-1.45); upper 1-sided 97.5% CI 1.29 percentage points vs 3.5 margin.
  • Physiological value 0.80 or less in 43.1% vs 35.9%; study lesion revascularized 41.2% vs 35.5%.

Caveat: Low event rate made the absolute margin lenient; post hoc relative-margin analysis failed noninferiority in mITT, passed per protocol. Investigator-initiated.

PositiveRCT, noninferiority, PROBEJ Am Coll Cardiol

Super-high-pressure noncompliant balloons vs intravascular lithotripsy in calcified lesions (VICTORY)

  • n=278; 68% severe calcium (OCT Fujino score 4 or more); median pressure 40 vs 6 atm.

Method and limits

  • Source: PubMed E-utilities, entry date within the last 7 days. Query A: 30 internal medicine and subspecialty journals. Query B: publication type Practice Guideline or Guideline in internal medicine topics. Duplicates removed by PMID.
  • Screening and summaries: Claude, from abstracts and metadata only. Numbers are copied from abstracts; open the linked record before citing.
  • Full text: PMC records exist for 14 of 139 candidates. A PMC record does not guarantee machine-readable full text.
  • Excluded by scope: oncology (including BCMA CAR-T and myeloma trials, FORTE, breast irradiation, French prostate cancer guideline), neurology (tau biomarkers, dementia), pediatrics (semaglutide in children, SURMOUNT-ADOLESCENTS, youth-onset type 1 diabetes food insecurity, Swiss paediatric home respiratory support, Powassan virus in children), obstetrics and gynecology (menopause, gestational diabetes, intrapartum trial, prenatal cfDNA), surgery-only (cardiac surgery delirium consensus, robotic pancreatoduodenectomy), ISHLT transplant nursing workforce statement, health policy, narrative and opinion pieces. R21/Matrix-M seasonal malaria mass vaccination trial (Lancet) skipped as a community vaccination campaign outside the internal medicine scope.
  • Not searched: preprints, society websites without PubMed indexing (ACC, ESC, KDIGO, IDSA, ADA living updates), FDA actions.
  • Badges describe the result against the primary end point: Positive, Negative, Neutral (no clear difference), Descriptive (observational, uncontrolled, or no efficacy test).
