Weekly brief of new internal medicine guidelines and research, summarized from PubMed abstracts.

2026-W46 2026-11-07 to 2026-11-14

  • Two large NEJM outcome trials were neutral: ziltivekimab (interleukin-6 (IL-6) inhibition, ZEUS) and pelacarsen (lipoprotein(a) lowering, Lp(a)HORIZON), with MACE hazard ratios (HR) of 0.99 and 1.02.
  • Systolic blood pressure (SBP) target below 120 mm Hg in type 2 diabetes: no MACE benefit in OPTIMAL-DIABETES (HR 0.90) and no cognitive benefit in BPROAD (HR 0.96).
  • OptimPacing (NEJM): left bundle-branch pacing beat right ventricular pacing in atrioventricular block (4-year composite 8.5% vs 19.2%; HR 0.41).

2026-W45 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-07

  • SHASTA-3/4 (NEJM): plozasiran cut triglycerides and acute pancreatitis (1 vs 5 per 100 patient-years) in severe hypertriglyceridemia.
  • PROGRESS (NEJM): transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR) beat surveillance in moderate aortic stenosis with at-risk features (16.7% vs 23.9% death or heart failure (HF) event at 2 years), driven by HF events.
  • SIMPLAAFY (JAMA): after left atrial appendage closure (LAAC), aspirin alone and reduced-dose non-vitamin K oral anticoagulant (NOAC) were both noninferior to dual antiplatelet therapy (DAPT) at 6 months.

2026-W44 2026-10-23 to 2026-10-30

  • IgA nephropathy: four NEJM phase 3 reports (sibeprenlimab, atacicept, povetacicept, ravulizumab) plus iptacopan long-term data; eGFR slopes stabilized on APRIL-directed agents.
  • LEAP-Block (JAMA): left bundle-branch area pacing beat right ventricular pacing on a composite driven by heart failure events (2.4% vs 12.5%).
  • AGA/AASLD issued a primary biliary cholangitis guideline (6 recommendations; PPAR agonists and linerixibat now included).
